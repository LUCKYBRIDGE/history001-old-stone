## 2. Upstream/downstream boundary

STYLE-GIR-V1 is a **style exploration anchor**, not an identity/object/world master.

Therefore the five candidates intentionally use:

- anonymous fictional human subject where a person is needed,
- non-diagnostic rough stone where hand/object contact is needed,
- non-canonical dawn environment vignette where world treatment is needed,
- low-specificity garment material,
- one anonymous moment for L/portrait equivalence.

They must **not** lock early:

- `ARU-IDENTITY-V1`,
- `DAMU-IDENTITY-V1`,
- `NUA-IDENTITY-V1`,
- `PLAYER-HUNT-BODY-V1`,
- `DAY1-HANDAXE-V1` morphology/fingerprint,
- `WORLD-CAMP-DAWN-A` geography,
- shelter footprint,
- route/landmark topology.

This prevents the style packet from silently reversing the approved lock order.
